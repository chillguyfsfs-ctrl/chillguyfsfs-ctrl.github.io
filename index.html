❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does paperlight work on Mac or Linux?

paperlight is built for Windows. Other operating systems are not supported right now.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. paperlight is fully offline once installed. It indexes and searches locally on your machine. This makes it great for specific work environments or private computers.

Are my files safe?

Completely. paperlight reads your document metadata and text to create its index. It never uploads, copies, modifies, or deletes your original files.

Is paperlight really free?

Yes. It is open-source software hosted on GitHub. Download, install, use — no payment, no account, no trial.

What if I have a lot of documents?

The indexer handles tens of thousands of files without slowing down. It combines modern search technology (SQLite FTS5) with a small, fast engine.
